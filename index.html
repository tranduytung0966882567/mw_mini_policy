Section 1

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MW Mini ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

1.1 Information We Collect & How We Use It

We do not require you to create an account, register an email, or sign in to use MW Mini. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information.

Key Commitment: All core feature data (Calendar events, Biorhythm calculations, Personal Notes, Draw & Art sketches, Expense records, and Scanned PDFs) are saved exclusively on your local device storage.

1.2 Device Permissions We Request

To deliver specific utility features, the App may request access to certain hardware and OS features. You can enable or revoke these at any time via your device settings:

Permission Feature / Purpose Data Handling
Camera Document scanning, AR Drawing trace overlay, and QR code reading. Processed in real-time in camera RAM. No footage is stored or transmitted to external servers.
Storage / Media Files Saving and exporting your drawings, PDF files, and backup data. Files are written directly to your chosen device folders or app private sandbox.
Microphone / Audio Mini Piano, Drum Pad sound synthesis, or audio recording features. Used strictly for instant sound playback or local audio effects. No audio is monitored or uploaded.
Sensors (Compass/Magnetometer) Direction heading and compass utility calculations. Sensor values are processed instantaneously on-device and never logged.

1.3 Third-Party SDKs and Services

The application may integrate reputable third-party services that process minimal non-personal identifiers (such as advertising IDs or crash logs) solely to keep the service free and stable:

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob: May serve non-personalized or personalized advertisements to support app maintenance. You can review Google's advertising privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Google ML Kit (Offline Translation & Barcode Scanning): ML model inference is executed on-device. Your scanned text and translation inputs are not sent to any cloud server.

1.4 On-Device & Offline Architecture

MW Mini is designed so that almost every core function—including lunar calendar algorithms, biorhythm mathematical models, note taking, drawing canvas, PDF viewing/signing, and offline phrasebook translation—works seamlessly without an active internet connection.

1.5 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application does not knowingly collect or solicit any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take necessary measures to remove any such data.
